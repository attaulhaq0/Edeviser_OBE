● NEW — RECOMMENDED
System 03

Unified Tactile — “One system, five accents”

Everything good from the prototype plus the missing piece: a role-accent spine, all within blue/green. One token set, one component library, but the accent follows the role — Student sky→teal, Teacher emerald, Coordinator blue→navy, Admin slate→navy, Parent cyan→teal. Student surfaces stay playful (characters, path); staff surfaces reuse the exact same cards and buttons in a restrained accent. The tactile press stays, tinted per role. No orange, no purple anywhere.

● NEW — FOR INSTITUTION SIDE
System 05

Editorial × Tactile — “Serious tables, satisfying actions”

The hybrid you asked for, purpose-built for the institution side (admin, coordinator, teacher). It keeps Editorial's density where staff live — Inter type, monospace numerals, hairline management tables, underline tabs — but swaps flat buttons for the prototype's tactile press and gives cards a gentle 13px lift. So a coordinator gets a precise attainment table, and the "Approve", "Open CQI", "Grade" buttons still feel physical and rewarding. Blue for coordinator/admin, emerald for teacher.

System 09

Neo Soft-3D — “Tactile depth”

A soft, sculpted direction where every surface looks moulded from one material — dual-tone soft shadows, extruded tiles, pressable controls that sink in, and gamified 3D icon tiles in blue/green. Use a subtle, restrained version for staff.
