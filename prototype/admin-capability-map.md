## Governance invariant

The effective autonomy level is the minimum of the institution, role, page, tool and user ceilings. Gulf Academy’s institution ceiling is A2. A tool may advertise an A3 maximum, but its effective level cannot exceed A2 and protected actions always require the named human approver. The prototype never presents draft-to-executed as a direct transition.
