# Teacher capability map

| Workspace | Question | Reads | Can prepare | Protected action / approver |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dashboard | What requires attention? | class summary, attention, grading, handoffs | intervention | activate or message / Teacher |
| Students | Who are my students? | roster, progress, course and CLO evidence | evidence summary | contact / Teacher |
| Student Triage | Who needs attention and why? | priorities, CLO, habit and consented Tutor evidence | check-in/intervention | activate or send / Teacher |
| Student detail | What explains Sarah’s learning state? | outcome, assignment, review and handoff evidence | check-in | send / Teacher |
| Handoffs | Which Tutor escalation requires me? | consented Tutor summary and CLO evidence | teacher response | send / Teacher |
| Curriculum Studio | Which CLO gap does content address? | course, module, outcome coverage | lesson/curriculum draft | publish / Teacher |
| Question Bank | Which questions should test the outcome? | CLO, module and assessment coverage | question draft | publish / Teacher |
| Rubric Builder | How should work be assessed? | course outcomes and criteria | rubric draft | save/publish / Teacher |
| Course Materials | What can students use? | course material inventory | material draft | publish / Teacher |
| Grading Queue | Why was feedback drafted? | rubric, submission and outcome evidence | feedback draft | change/release feedback or grade / Teacher |
| Gradebook | What evidence contributes to CLO state? | scores and outcome evidence | evidence summary | change grade / Teacher |
| Attendance | What did I observe today? | assigned class roster | follow-up reminder | alter attendance / Teacher |
| Profile | What is E Deviser allowed to prepare? | teacher responsibilities/preferences | configured drafts | all academic actions / Teacher |

Operational autonomy is **A2 · Suggest & Draft**. Mastery, Habit, Tutor, Intervention and Teacher Agents may prepare concise, evidence-checked recommendations; Evidence Check validates contributing signals and Policy Check marks approval requirements. No agent sends, publishes, grades, releases, or records attendance.
