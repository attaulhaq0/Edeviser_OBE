Composite index vs two single-column indexes?

by Layla · yesterday

When should I use a composite index instead of creating two separate single-column indexes on the same table?

A

Prof. Ahmed ✓ Answer

Use a composite index when your queries filter on both columns together — it's more efficient than two separate indexes for that specific combination, since the DB can use one index scan instead of two.
