What is the Duolingo design system?

The Duolingo playbook — and our whole app in it

Duolingo's design language turns learning into a game you don't want to break. It's built on a few repeatable ideas: a friendly character who reacts to you, bite-size steps, instant feedback, and a visible streak you protect daily. Below are its core principles (each with how we'd adapt it to Edeviser in blue-green, never orange/purple), then every role's dashboard rebuilt in this style so you can see exactly how the product would feel.

🦉

Characters everywhere

A mascot guides, praises and gently nags — the emotional core.

Ours: Edu (owl), Pip (fox), Sage, Nova — teal/sky palette.
🧩

Bite-size steps

Short lessons on a visual path; one clear next action.

Ours: Learning Path nodes ordered by Bloom's level.
⚡

Instant feedback

Every tap answers back with sound, color and motion.

Ours: XP pops, correct/again states, tutor hints.
🔥

Streaks & XP & Leagues

Daily streak, XP, leagues create a habit loop.

Ours: streak + freeze, XP schedule, league tiers.
👆

Chunky 3D buttons

Big rounded buttons with a bottom edge that presses in.

Ours: our btn3d, tinted per role, blue/green.
🅰️

Bold rounded type

Friendly heavy rounded font; huge numbers; little text.

Ours: Baloo 2 headings + Nunito body.
🎉

Celebrate wins

Confetti, badges and level-ups reward every milestone.

Ours: confetti + badge-pop, well-being safe.
💚

Forgiving & kind

Mistakes are low-stakes; encouragement over punishment.

Ours: comeback challenges, gentle nudges, no shame.
🎓
