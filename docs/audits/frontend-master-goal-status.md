## Dependency authorization

The user has explicitly allowed installing libraries from the researched direction when they are genuinely needed and will be used. Apply this to justified, compatible dependencies—not the entire shortlist. Existing Recharts/TanStack Table remain the default. Selective virtualization, specialist visualization or explorer tooling can be added when an actual requirement warrants it. Record licensing, version/peer compatibility, delivery cost, ownership and rollback. Installing a package is not permission to purchase a commercial license or claim Enterprise features are licensed.

No new visualization engine or commercial license was installed/purchased during the research. Storybook, a token compiler and the optional registry/MCP infrastructure are not being represented as already installed.

## Why this is not finished, and when it will be

The scope grew from defect repair to a product-wide redesign plus an approved architecture program. Early work uncovered unreliable tests and real ownership/font/cascade defects that had to be corrected before their passing results were trustworthy. Shared controls improved, but many route bodies and architecture deliverables have not yet received their complete implementation/verification pass. Recent source/browser checks continued to reveal real issues: initial chart measurement warnings, table pagination mode switching with unknown counts, row-action identity, and malformed test fixtures. Fixing those rather than waiving them is necessary, but does not make the remaining rollout small.

**There is no defensible completion date yet.** The60-round continuation cap is not a deadline or a project percentage. It would be misleading to promise completion after one more suite, a few rounds, or a particular day while major workstreams remain unstarted and visual review is unavailable in the current model. The next concrete checkpoint is the combined Course/KPI/chart source and browser acceptance. A reliable calendar forecast requires a bounded remaining route/component work breakdown and an available visual-review path, not an invented percentage.

Completion means required findings and approved architectural deliverables are resolved or supported by an evidence-backed optional-tool decision, all required current-source checks pass, route/state coverage and visual/performance acceptance are complete, and the release/rollback handoff is reproducible. Production deployment/attestation remains a separate authorized step. Until then, keep the master goal active and report progress without calling the product100% finished.
