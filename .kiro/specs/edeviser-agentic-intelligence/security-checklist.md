## Certification sweeps (agentic spec tasks 4.8 / 5.4 / 6.4 / 7.3)

`src/__tests__/properties/agentCertification.property.test.ts` (fast-check, ≥100 iterations each):

- **7.3 — A3 never bypasses approval**: for every PROTECTED_ACTION × risk × autonomy level, `mayAutoExecute` is always false; effective autonomy is the strict minimum of all ceilings (user preference can lower, never raise).
- **4.8 — no invented evidence**: risk findings carrying numeric score fields are rejected; habit signals without evidence citations are rejected; intervention drafts without `approvalRequired: true` are rejected.
- **6.4 / 5.4 — role-scoped tool surfaces**: every registered read tool is read-only by name; the student tool surface excludes governance tools; `SPECIALISTS_BY_ROLE` never grants admin/coordinator specialists to parent/student; outcome-governance write tools are admin-only and always produce approval proposals.
